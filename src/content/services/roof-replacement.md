## About the service

A roof replacement is a major decision. You should not have to make it based on pressure, guesswork, or a rushed opinion from someone who only looked from the driveway.

J Land Contracting starts with an inspection. We look at the roof condition, visible damage, age-related wear, flashing, gutters, ventilation concerns, and signs of leaks. Then we explain what we found in plain language and recommend the next step.

A replacement may make sense when shingles are missing, curling, cracking, shedding granules, or no longer keeping water out. It may also be the right path after wind, hail, falling debris, or repeated leak repairs. If the damage may be insurance-related, we help document it and communicate with your insurance provider.

## Why it matters

A failing roof can lead to damaged decking, insulation problems, interior leaks, and mold concerns. Waiting too long usually makes the repair more expensive.

Our job is to help you understand whether replacement is truly needed and, if it is, to coordinate the work clearly from the first inspection through cleanup.

## How to get started

1. **Schedule a free inspection** — We inspect the roof and document what we find.
2. **Review the recommendation** — We explain whether repair or replacement makes the most sense.
3. **Discuss insurance documentation** — If storm damage is involved, we help organize the claim information.
4. **Coordinate the work** — We help manage the schedule, materials, installation, and cleanup.

## Expected result

You get a roof built to protect your home, plus a clear record of the work performed and the reason it was recommended. No inflated claims. No pressure. Just a straight path from inspection to finished roof.
