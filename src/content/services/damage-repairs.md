## About the service

Storm damage is not always obvious from the ground. Wind can lift shingles. Hail can bruise the surface. Falling limbs can damage decking or flashing. Water can find a small opening and show up inside the home days or weeks later.

J Land Contracting inspects the roof, documents visible damage, and explains what needs immediate attention. If the roof is exposed or actively leaking, the first priority is stopping further damage. After that, we help coordinate the repair plan and insurance communication when a claim may apply.

## Why it matters

Storm damage gets worse when it is ignored. A missing shingle can become a leak. A leak can damage decking, insulation, drywall, and personal property. Quick documentation also matters because insurance claims have deadlines and require clear evidence.

## How to get started

1. **Call after the storm** — Contact us if you see missing shingles, leaks, fallen limbs, gutter damage, or anything unusual.
2. **Schedule a roof inspection** — We inspect visible and hard-to-see areas and take photos.
3. **Secure urgent problems** — If the roof needs temporary protection, we help coordinate that first.
4. **Document and repair** — We help organize the claim information when needed and coordinate the repair work.

## Expected result

You get a clear picture of the storm damage, a plan to protect the home, and help moving from inspection to repair without being left alone in the insurance process.
