## About the service

A roof inspection should give you answers, not a sales pitch. J Land Contracting offers free roof inspections for homeowners who want to know whether their roof is sound, damaged, aging, leaking, or ready for repair.

We inspect the roof surface, flashing, gutters, visible ventilation issues, storm damage signs, and interior leak indicators when accessible. If we find damage that may be insurance-related, we document it with photos and explain the next step.

## Why it matters

Most roof problems are easier to fix when they are caught early. By the time a ceiling stain appears, the roof may have been letting in water for a while. An inspection helps you understand the condition of the roof before the problem grows.

## How to get started

1. **Request an inspection** — Call (410) 292-0801 or use the form on this page.
2. **We schedule a visit** — We set a time to inspect the roof and related areas.
3. **We document what we find** — Photos and plain-language notes help you understand the roof condition.
4. **You get a recommendation** — If repair, replacement, or insurance documentation makes sense, we explain why. If the roof looks fine, we tell you that too.

## Expected result

You leave with a clear understanding of the roof, what needs attention, and what can wait. That is the whole point of the inspection.
