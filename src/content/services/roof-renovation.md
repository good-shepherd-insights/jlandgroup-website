## About the service

Roof renovation is for roofs that need real attention but may not need a full replacement. That can mean replacing damaged sections, correcting flashing issues, improving ventilation, repairing leak-prone areas, or restoring parts of the roof that have worn unevenly.

J Land Contracting starts with an inspection so we can separate urgent problems from normal wear. If a repair or renovation is enough, we will say that. If replacement is the better long-term option, we will explain why.

## Why it matters

Small roof problems rarely stay small. Loose flashing, lifted shingles, poor drainage, and ventilation issues can lead to leaks and damaged decking over time. A focused renovation can protect the home and buy more life from a roof that still has solid structure left.

## How to get started

1. **Schedule an inspection** — We check the roof surface, flashing, gutters, vents, and signs of interior moisture.
2. **Review the findings** — We show what needs attention and what does not.
3. **Choose the right scope** — We help decide whether renovation, repair, or replacement is the best fit.
4. **Complete the work** — We coordinate the materials, crew, schedule, and cleanup.

## Expected result

You get a roof that is repaired where it needs repair, without turning every concern into a full replacement. The goal is simple: protect the home and make the right call for the roof in front of us.
