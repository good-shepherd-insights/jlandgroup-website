## About the service

Commercial roofs need careful planning because the building, tenants, inventory, employees, and daily operations may all be affected. J Land Contracting helps property owners and managers understand what is happening on the roof and what should happen next.

We inspect commercial roof concerns, document damage, coordinate repair or replacement planning, and help with insurance communication when storm damage may be involved.

## Why it matters

A roof problem at a commercial property can interrupt business quickly. Water intrusion can damage interiors, create safety concerns, and lead to expensive downtime. A clear inspection and repair plan helps protect the property before a small issue becomes a larger disruption.

## How to get started

1. **Request an assessment** — We inspect the roof and document current conditions.
2. **Review the scope** — We explain what needs repair, what can wait, and what may require replacement.
3. **Coordinate insurance documentation** — If storm damage is involved, we help organize the evidence and communication.
4. **Plan the work** — We coordinate the schedule and project details with the goal of reducing disruption to the business.

## Expected result

You get a practical commercial roofing plan, clear documentation, and a contractor who communicates through the process instead of leaving you guessing.
