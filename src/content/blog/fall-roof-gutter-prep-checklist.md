## Homeowner question

Prepare roof and gutter observations before leaves, rain, and colder weather.

## Short answer

A seasonal checklist should help homeowners notice visible issues early while staying safe. This draft focuses on ground-level and indoor observations, clear documentation, and conservative next steps.

## Safe seasonal checklist

- Look for visible missing, loose, or shifted roof materials from the ground.
- Check gutters and downspouts for overflow signs, sagging, separation, or blocked discharge paths.
- Look indoors for ceiling stains, damp areas, or repeated moisture signs.
- Photograph issues from safe locations and note the date/weather context.
- Do not climb on the roof or perform risky work because of this checklist.
