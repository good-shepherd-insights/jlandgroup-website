## Short answer

A visible roof-related symptom can have more than one cause. This draft helps homeowners document what they see, avoid unsafe inspection behavior, and understand when a qualified professional review may be appropriate.

## What the symptom may indicate

This page may discuss possible roof, drainage, ventilation, weather, or interior causes only as general possibilities. It must not claim that a symptom proves a specific cause without inspection.

## Safe homeowner documentation checklist

- Take photos from the ground or safe indoor areas.
- Note date, weather timing, location, and whether the condition repeats.
- Protect belongings from active water where possible.
- Avoid climbing onto a wet, steep, icy, damaged, or unfamiliar roof.
- Save questions for the inspection rather than attempting risky diagnosis.
