## Homeowner question

Understand when a roof issue may call for repair versus a full replacement conversation.

## Short answer

A useful roofing service page should help a homeowner understand the decision path, not pressure them into a pre-set answer. This draft explains what a homeowner can observe safely, what a professional may need to review, and which claims must be verified before any public use.

## What homeowners can safely document

- Where the concern appears from the ground or inside the home.
- When the issue was first noticed and whether it changes after rain or wind.
- Visible stains, missing materials, loose debris, or recurring trouble spots.
- Photos taken from safe locations only; no roof climbing is recommended in this draft.

## JLand-style review path

1. Intake the homeowner concern and timing.
2. Review visible exterior indicators where safe and appropriate.
3. Check interior or attic indicators only where safe and permitted.
4. Separate isolated repair possibilities from broader roof-system concerns.
5. Provide a recommendation that avoids unsupported price, warranty, insurance, or timeline promises.
